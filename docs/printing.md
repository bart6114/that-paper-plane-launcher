# Printing and assembly notes

Saved profile: P1S, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers, six walls, six top and bottom layers, 45% gyroid, automatic tree supports. The slider overrides walls to ten and adds a 5 mm outer brim. Select your actual PLA or PLA Matte filament before slicing.

Estimates come from Bambu Studio 02.08.02.61 and may change with filament selection or slicer version. The body plate needs no support in this saved layout. Small parts still use some support. No G-code is supplied.

Use the complete project for a new set. The replacement projects are intended for the current matching assembly; the base-only replacement retains the existing cover and small parts.

Remove support and brim carefully. Keep the slider contact surfaces smooth and avoid enlarging the catch pocket during cleanup. Follow the saved placement: the slider stands upright and the body halves lie on their exterior faces.

The integral posts pass into the cover bores and finish flush with its exterior. The printed keys and retaining pins secure the shell joints; do not substitute adhesive in moving bearings. Route the small return elastic through the provided guides so it cannot be pinched between the halves. Band size/tension was not measured; prior modeling assumed a return band up to 3 mm wide by 1.5 mm thick.

Before adding the main launch bands, check that both shells seat fully, the slider travels freely, and the trigger/catch can load, hold, release, and reset. Actual operation is not established by the digital checks. Use paper planes and keep the launch path clear of people.
